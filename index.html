00 · The beans · Age 3

It started with dancing.

Before I could draw a straight line, I was moving to whatever was playing. That restless, make-something energy is still the raw bean of everything I do.

01 · The grind

Then I picked up a pencil.

Dancing turned into doodling, and doodling turned into drawing. Hours of it. The grind is where I learned that craft is mostly showing up, again and again.

02 · The bloom · 2020

A lockdown, a laptop, a cause.

During COVID I taught myself graphic design to make visuals for a volunteering role. What started as helping out bloomed into the thing I couldn't stop doing.

03 · The pour · College

Neuroscience, then code.

I came to college for neuroscience, to understand how people think. I switched to computer science to build for them. Somewhere in that pour, product design became the point.

04 · The cup · Now

Product design, served.

Today I design products that feel like a good cup: made from scratch, made with care, and made for the person holding it. Here's what's been brewing.
